Why it works

Feeds keep you up by asking "what's next?". A train doesn't ask anything.

Night Train pulls you in the way a window does: rhythm, continuity, a little progress — never a jolt. It is inspired by the "cognitive shuffle": a slow stream of neutral, unrelated images that gives a racing mind somewhere gentle to go.

An infinite line

Forests, a coastline, fields, small lit towns and named stations — every kilometre generated, never repeated.

Things happen, softly

Tunnels. A bridge over moonlit water. A train going the other way. A deer at the tree line, a fox running alongside, an owl on the wire. Distant lightning on a rainy stretch.

It lets you go

The screen dims and warms, the train eases off, the sound keeps rolling after your phone locks, then fades on a timer you choose.

Questions
Is it a game?

Not really. There is nothing to win and nothing to finish. It's closer to a place than a game — a seat by a window that keeps moving.

Does it keep playing when I lock my phone?

Yes. The sound continues in the background and fades out after your sleep timer (15–90 minutes, or never).

Does it track my sleep?

No sensors, no microphone. It simply notices when you've stopped touching the screen for a while, and remembers where the train was.

Is it a treatment for insomnia?

No. Night Train is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If sleep is a persistent problem, please talk to a doctor.
