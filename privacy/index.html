Night Train — Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 September 2026 · Operator: TanCode, Türkiye, ahmetaytanozu@gmail.com

Night Train is designed to know as little about you as possible.

What the app stores

Everything Night Train remembers is stored on your device only: your route, your journal of nights, your sound and sleep timer preferences. There are no accounts, and we do not show ads. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

One small thing outlives the app. So that the three free nights are three free nights and not three every time the app is installed again, the app keeps a list of the calendar dates on which you opened it. On iOS that list is written to the system keychain, which the phone keeps after the app is deleted; on Android it can come back through the phone's own backup. It is dates and nothing else. It never leaves your device, it is not sent to us or to anyone, and it does not identify you. Buying Night Train Plus makes it irrelevant, and resetting your phone or clearing its keychain removes it.

What the app counts

We use Google Firebase Analytics to see where people stop using the app: how many finish the three opening pages, how many nights they ride, whether they find the things the window does, when the price is shown and whether a purchase goes through. It is counts and the names of those moments, nothing else. We do not send your route, your journal, your postcards, anything you type, or your location. There is no advertising identifier and nothing is used to follow you into other apps or websites. Firebase gives the app an anonymous instance identifier so the same phone is not counted twice; it is not your name and we cannot turn it back into you.

We also use Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes it sends the technical report of that crash: where in the code it happened, the version of the app, the model of phone and the version of its operating system. It does not send your journal, your route or anything you wrote. A crash is the reason people leave without telling you, so this is the only way we find out.

Firebase's privacy terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases

Night Train Plus is a single purchase, paid once. It is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms. We use RevenueCat to validate the purchase and unlock the app. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, the purchase receipt, your device platform and app version. It does not receive your name, email or contacts. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, precise device identifiers, advertising identifiers.

Children

Night Train is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete beyond the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat and the stores. To request its deletion or ask anything else, write to ahmetaytanozu@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at https://aytanozu.github.io/nighttrain-site/privacy/ with a new effective date.
