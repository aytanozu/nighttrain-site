Night Train — Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 August 2026 · Operator: TanCode, Türkiye, ahmetaytanozu@gmail.com

Night Train is designed to know as little about you as possible.

What the app stores

Everything Night Train remembers — your route, your journal of nights, your sound and sleep-timer preferences — is stored on your device only. There are no accounts. We do not run analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking technologies. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Night Train Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock the subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, the purchase receipt, your device platform and app version. It does not receive your name, email or contacts. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, precise device identifiers, advertising identifiers.

Children

Night Train is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete beyond the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat and the stores. To request its deletion or ask anything else, write to ahmetaytanozu@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at https://aytanozu.github.io/nighttrain-site/privacy/ with a new effective date.
